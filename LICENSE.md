# Licence — not yet chosen

This project is **not yet licensed for reuse**. No permission to use, copy, modify
or distribute is granted at this stage.

The intended licence is **Apache-2.0**, chosen for its patent grant and clarity
around commercial reuse. It will be applied once two things are done:

1. A dependency licence audit of everything the reference implementation pulls in.
2. A provenance review confirming no adapted third-party code carries incompatible
   terms.

Alternatives still on the table: **MIT** if simplicity matters more than the patent
language, or **AGPL-3.0** if hosted modifications should be required to stay open —
at a known cost in adoption.

Any bundled fixture assets must be generated or verifiably public domain. Book
cover images from third-party metadata providers must not be redistributed here
without reviewing their individual rights.
